# Wispr Flow

**What it is:** A voice-to-text dictation app that turns natural speech into
clean, AI-formatted writing inside any app on Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android.
In 2026 the company also shipped Wispr Notetaker, an AI meeting notes
feature that captures, transcribes, and summarizes meetings.

**Problem it solves:** Lets a solo developer write PR descriptions, commit
messages, issue writeups, comments, and AI-coding prompts by speaking, at
roughly four times typing speed, with filler words and false starts cleaned
up automatically.

**When I'd reach for it:**

- Drafting long-form prose (issue writeups, PR explanations, docs) faster than typing allows.
- Feeding half-formed plans to an AI coding assistant in Cursor or VS Code by voice.
- Reducing keyboard load when wrist strain or RSI makes heavy typing painful.

**When I wouldn't:**

- Confidential code or client data under NDA, since audio and active-window screenshots are sent to cloud servers.
- Offline work or flaky connections, because there is no local processing mode.

**Pricing posture:** Free tier capped at 2,000 words per week on Mac/Windows
(unlimited on Android during a 2026 promo); Pro is $15/month or $12/month
billed annually for unlimited words; Growth is $23/month or $18/month billed
annually; Enterprise is custom and sales-led. Notetaker ships on Growth and
above.

**Reality check:** All transcription is cloud-only, and reviewers flag that
the context-aware feature captures periodic active-window screenshots, a
structural dealbreaker for sensitive work. Wispr trains on your data by
default on Free and Pro plans — opt out in Settings before using it with
anything sensitive. Memory footprint on older hardware is meaningful (reports
of ~1 GB while recording on 8 GB machines). Independent reviews report a
2.7/5 Trustpilot score. Notetaker transcription is strong but misses speaker
labels and doesn't keep meeting audio for verification. Local-first tools
like SuperWhisper win on privacy.
