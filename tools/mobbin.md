# Mobbin

**What it is:** A searchable library of real, shipped mobile and web app screenshots organized into step-by-step user flows, built for design pattern research.

**Problem it solves:** When you are designing onboarding, a paywall, or a checkout for a side project and want to copy proven patterns instead of inventing them, Mobbin gives you dozens of real examples in seconds.

**When I'd reach for it:**

- Designing a common flow (onboarding, subscription paywall, settings) and wanting modern patterns rather than guessing.
- Running a quick competitor teardown to see how similar apps handle a screen.
- Settling a UI debate with real shipped evidence instead of opinion.

**When I wouldn't:**

- Branding, print, or marketing-site work with minimal interaction design.
- As a substitute for actual user research; it shows what others built, not what your users need.

**Pricing posture:** Free tier exists but is restrictive (latest 4 apps, 3 collections, limited search). Pro is roughly $10/month billed yearly; Team is about $12 per member per month.

**Reality check:** Reviewers in 2026 consistently call it the broadest flow-based library but flag recurring gripes: the free tier is intentionally crippled, there is no true monthly plan so you are pushed to annual billing, search relies on filters with no natural-language AI, and content is screenshot-only with thinner web coverage. Alternatives like Refero and InspoAI compete on AI search and broader assets.
