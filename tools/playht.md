# PlayHT

**What it is:** A now-defunct text-to-speech API that offered 800+ voices across 142 languages, voice cloning from 30 seconds of audio, and a Turbo model for real-time applications.

**Problem it solves:** N/A — service is shut down.

**When I'd reach for it:**

- Do not use: PlayHT shut down permanently on December 31, 2025; migrate to ElevenLabs, Cartesia, or Deepgram Audio for comparable TTS capabilities.

**When I wouldn't:**

- Always — the service no longer exists.

**Pricing posture:** No longer available. Domain (play.ht) stopped resolving July 2026.

**Reality check:** Meta acquired the PlayHT / PlayAI team in July 2025 in an acqui-hire for approximately 35 people who joined Meta Superintelligence Labs. The API went dark on July 26, 2025 — weeks ahead of the announced shutdown timeline — breaking integrations overnight. All products (the studio, the API, Voice Agents) closed permanently on December 31, 2025 with no data export tooling for saved audio or voice clones. The brand was retired entirely; Meta is using the technology internally for AI Characters and wearable products, not as a public TTS service. If you were using PlayHT, ElevenLabs and Cartesia are the closest production-grade alternatives.
